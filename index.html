About Me

Fired twice. Betting on myself anyway.

I'm OnNae Barlow, and I spent seven years in a corporate finance career: steady, secure, and slowly not enough. I've been obsessed with business since I was fifteen: reading the books, listening to the podcasts, starting little ventures on the side of full-time jobs, just to feel closer to it.

Then, at the gym one day, I found Shelby Sapp. I fell down the rabbit hole of her content and couldn't stop. Someone was actually teaching people how to get paid well for helping other businesses grow. I signed up for She Sells Academy on the spot.

Two weeks into the course, I got fired. Again.

It was the second time in my career: six years at the first company, exactly one year at the next, and then gone. Most people would've called that a sign to slow down. I called it permission to stop hedging.

I'm currently completing She Sells Academy, and I'm building Techedge Innovation to do exactly what I went looking for: get on calls, close real deals, and get paid for the results, not the hours. When I'm not on a call, I'm probably in the mountains or strapped to a snowboard.

Before You Book

Is this a fit?

I take on a limited number of engagements at a time, so I keep the calendar for companies where this genuinely works both ways.

  • You have a defined offer that's already live and selling, not still being validated
  • You can provide consistent leads. I'm not building your pipeline from zero
  • You're able to structure pay as commission, or commission plus a small retainer
  • You can share basic call data so we can track close rate and show rate accurately
  • You're looking for a long-term closing partner, not a single one-off favor
